🔧 Why can't I speak? How to fix microphone in Chrome / Edge:
  1. Look at your browser address bar at the very top (left of localhost:5000).
  2. Click the Padlock (🔒) or Site settings (⚙️) button.
  3. Find Microphone in permissions and switch it to "Allow".
  4. Windows Users: Make sure Windows Settings > Privacy > Microphone > Allow apps to access your microphone is ON.
  5. Click "Request / Test Microphone Access" above to confirm.
