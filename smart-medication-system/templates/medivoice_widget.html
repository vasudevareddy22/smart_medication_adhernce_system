🔧 How to enable microphone in Google Chrome:
  1. Look at Chrome's address bar next to http://127.0.0.1:5000 or http://localhost:5000.
  2. Click the Tune / Settings icon (🎛️) or Padlock icon (🔒) on the left side of the URL.
  3. Locate Microphone and toggle/change the switch to "Allow".
  4. If blocked globally, open Chrome menu > Settings > Privacy & Security > Site settings > Microphone and allow this site.
  5. Ensure Windows 10/11 Microphone access is enabled: Windows Settings > Privacy > Microphone > "Allow desktop apps to access your microphone".
  6. Click "Request / Test Microphone Access" above to verify your hardware track.
